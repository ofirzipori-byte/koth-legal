Privacy Policy

King of the Hill
Developer: OnFIReZ
Last updated: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how King of the Hill (the “Game”) handles information when you play. The Game provides online multiplayer functionality using Epic Online Services (“EOS”).

1. Anonymous Player Identity

When you first launch the Game, it automatically creates an anonymous EOS Connect Device ID identity. EOS assigns a Product User ID (“PUID”) to that identity. The PUID is used by the Game and EOS to identify players and provide online multiplayer features, including lobbies and peer-to-peer connections.

The Game does not ask you to register, provide an email address or phone number, create a password, or link an external account. The anonymous identity uses local device credentials and does not provide cross-device account access.

2. Player and Gameplay Information

You can choose an in-game nickname, character, and player color. The Game processes and exchanges information needed to run a multiplayer match, including player identifiers, lobby information, player positions, scores, cards, game actions, and connection status.

Your in-game nickname, selected character, and player color are visible to other participants in your multiplayer session. Gameplay information, including player positions, scores, card plays, and game actions, is also shared with other participants.

For your privacy, we recommend choosing a nickname that does not include your full name, contact details, or other personally identifying information.

3. Device and Technical Information

When creating an EOS Device ID, the Game provides EOS with the device’s operating-system name and device model.

EOS and Epic may also process technical and network information needed to provide their services. This may include IP addresses, device identifiers, operating-system information, network connection details, and service diagnostics.

Epic’s services may use IP addresses to infer general location information. The Game does not request access to your device’s GPS location.

EOS may also collect service-quality metrics, such as randomly generated session identifiers, API request counts, latency measurements, and success or error status codes, to provide, maintain, and troubleshoot EOS services.

For more information about Epic’s information practices, please review the Epic Games Privacy Policy.

4. Information Stored on Your Device

The Game stores certain preferences locally on your device, including your chosen nickname and the most recently used room code. These settings are not maintained in a separate user database operated by the developer.

5. How Information Is Used

Information processed by the Game and EOS is used to:

  • Create and maintain your anonymous player identity.
  • Identify players during multiplayer sessions.
  • Create, discover, and join multiplayer lobbies.
  • Establish peer-to-peer connections between players.
  • Synchronize gameplay information and display player details.
  • Maintain local game preferences.
  • Maintain the security, reliability, and proper operation of the Game and EOS services.

The current version of the Game does not use player information for advertising or targeted marketing. The Game does not include a separate third-party advertising or analytics provider.

6. Epic Online Services and Information Sharing

The Game relies on Epic Online Services for its online identity and multiplayer infrastructure. The developer does not operate a separate game backend or maintain an independent database of player accounts.

Information required to provide EOS services is transmitted to and processed by Epic. The Game uses EOS peer-to-peer networking for multiplayer communication. Depending on the connection, network traffic may be transmitted directly between players or through EOS network infrastructure.

Information needed to participate in a match is shared with other participants as described in Section 2. Epic processes information under its own privacy policy and applicable EOS agreements. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the Game, its players, or legal rights.

7. Storage and Retention

Locally stored preferences, such as your nickname and last room code, remain on your device until they are changed, the application’s data is cleared, or the Game is uninstalled.

According to Epic’s EOS documentation, uninstalling the application on Android and iOS automatically removes the local Device ID credentials created by the application. Removing local credentials does not necessarily delete information already processed or retained by EOS.

EOS service-side information is handled and retained by Epic according to its own policies, operational requirements, and applicable legal obligations. The developer does not control Epic’s specific retention periods.

If local Device ID credentials are lost, the previous anonymous identity may no longer be accessible. Reinstalling the Game may result in the creation of a new anonymous identity.

The Game does not use EOS Player Data Storage to store player saves or progression, and does not use EOS Stats, Achievements, or Leaderboards as player-facing features.

8. Data Security

The Game relies on the security and encryption mechanisms provided by Epic Online Services and the underlying mobile platforms for online communications. We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the Game. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. International Data Processing

Epic operates services internationally. Information processed through EOS may be stored or transferred outside your country of residence, including to countries where Epic or its service providers operate. Please refer to the Epic Games Privacy Policy for more information.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game’s features, services, or data practices change. The latest version will be published at the Privacy Policy link provided in the Game and on the relevant app store listing.
